# Code of Conduct

We want CookOps to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Participants must:

- treat people with respect and assume good faith;
- use inclusive language and welcome differing viewpoints;
- focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people; and
- accept correction and help keep discussions useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's available private contact channel. Please include enough context
to investigate and keep reports confidential.
